Full Description

This beautifully upgraded corner-lot home sits on one of the best streets in The Breakers, Pointe Marin's premier Shea Homes development. Spanning 3,853 square feet across two stories, the home offers 4 bedrooms and 5 bathrooms, three fireplaces, and a chef's kitchen with an island, breakfast area, and pantry. The main level includes a formal dining room, family room, and guest quarters, while the upper level features the primary suite, additional bedrooms, and a media room. Outside, enjoy an enclosed patio, fully landscaped front and back yards with automatic sprinklers, and a three-car garage. Recent upgrades throughout make this move-in ready.
